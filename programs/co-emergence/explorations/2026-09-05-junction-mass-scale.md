## 3. The convergent negative, stated precisely

The three surviving negatives are one statement seen from three sides. Stated so it can be attacked again:

> **(Sketch as a whole; components labelled.)** Let the junction be a no-boundary Euclidean cap with free matter, glued to a Lorentzian branch either by analytic continuation (Hartle–Hawking) or as a smooth degenerate surface (the SCB construction with odd degeneracy order n). Then:
> 1. *(Rigorous by citation — HH83, HH85, HHH08.)* The state on Σ contributes no continuous parameter beyond the action's couplings. Halliwell–Hawking's inhomogeneous modes start in the Euclidean ground state; Hartle–Hawking–Hertog's branch label φ₀ is a modulus whose scales are those of V(φ), imported; the no-boundary weight favors the lowest available H, not an exponentially separated one.
> 2. *(Rigorous, computed.)* The same-boundary saddle-ratio parameter c(a) = (1 − H²a²)^{3/2} vanishes at the K = 0 junction and is a phase on the Lorentzian side, so saddle multiplicity yields no exponential hierarchy for round-S³ junctions. (Sketch for the topology sum.)
> 3. *(Rigorous given fixed background and smoothness.)* K_Σ = 0 is forced in both gluings — by the reality condition in HH (FLRW, pure Λ or φ at an extremum of V; with a generic scalar the saddles are complex and there is no real junction at all), and by Hayward / Kossowski–Kriele and by curvature regularity in the smooth SCB class (§6c). Extrinsic curvature is not a modulus.
> 4. *(Rigorous.)* The lapse degeneracy order n is a diffeomorphism invariant (the proper-time chart is not a chart of the same smooth structure), an odd integer in the smooth class, and inert: every Level-2/3 quantity (mode functions, junction matching, on-shell action, curvature invariants) is n-independent in proper variables. It is Level-1 data, and it cannot feed a coupling.
> 5. *(Rigorous, standard QFT.)* An exponentially separated scale can live on a branch only as the transmuted scale Λ = μ e^{−8π²/(b₀g²(μ))} of an asymptotically free sector, which is RG-invariant and independent of signature, foliation and state. The cap depends on g² perturbatively (monotone at leading order) and on Λ_QCD itself only through (Λ_QCD/H_J)^{b₀} ~ 10⁻¹²⁰; hence the cap neither selects g² nor obstructs transmutation. Matching the hadronic scale needs α_s(H_J) ∈ [1/50, 1/44], sensitivity ≈ 2.5% per e-fold of Λ_QCD; no combination of GΛ, ln(3π/GΛ), a, c yields the exponent 40–45 (the closest, ln(1/GΛ) ≈ 13, is short by a factor 3, twelve orders in the scale).

Bridging 60 orders with any universal O(1) ratio needs depth of order 10² (Feigenbaum's δ needs 90; e² needs 69); no tree structure examined supplies such a depth with a selection rule.
>
> **Hence the junction carries exactly one continuous dimensionless number, ε_J = f(a₂), and every other scale is M_P × (function of a coupling). A mass hierarchy requires a selection principle for a coupling. The record contains none.**

## 4. Shared-premise check: convergence is evidence, not proof

Per the routine's own lesson from its first cycle, three independent negatives agreeing is not the same as three independent verifications. Two premises were shared:

**Premise 1 — G (equivalently M_P) is an undischarged input.** Disclosed by A and inherited by all three. It is the framework's own position at Level 2 (the semiclassical Einstein equation carries G), and the gravitational Coleman–Weinberg literature verified by B shows what discharging it would look like (Álvarez-Luna et al. 2023: M_P² = ξ⟨φ⟩², with the scalar a loop factor below M_P — it answers "where does M_P come from," not "why is m ≪ M_P"). No blind spot here, but the mass test is, at bottom, a test of whether anything other than M_P and integers is input.

**Premise 2 — "the junction" is the anomaly-driven de Sitter fixed point.** This one was unexamined by all three attempts and is the cycle's main structural finding. Two facts on record bear on it:

- The exact constant-H solution is unstable, with a quasi-de Sitter lifetime of 10⁻⁴²–10⁻⁴⁰ s (`fpe-fixed-point-is-inflationary`), and the program already gates a claim about the state at that fixed point as "INDEPENDENTLY FATAL as a physical claim" for exactly this reason (`ce-euclidean-vacuum-at-fixed-point`). A Lorentzian branch "at H_J" has no operational content for particle masses; every mass-relevant regime is reached only through the instability and reheating, dynamics the fixed-point program explicitly does not use.
- For Standard-Model field content, a₂ = (N_S + 11N_F + 62N_V)/2 ≈ 498 (N_S = 4, N_F = 22.5, N_V = 12), giving H_J/M_P ≈ 1.07 under the printed coefficient 180π/(G a₂) and ≈ 0.045 under the canonical H ~ M_P/√N — inside or within about an order of magnitude of the Planck scale, outside semiclassical validity. The recorded range a₂ ∈ [10³, 10⁵] presupposes hundreds of additional fields (Hawking–Hertog–Reall's "sufficiently many matter fields"). The attack pass on C checked the printed coefficient against Duff 1994 eq. 31 and found it the standard anomaly formula; the recorded 16π discrepancy with Linde's form (`fpe-starobinsky-coefficient`, #168) may decompose as 8π (units) × 2 (definition of k₂ vs a₂) — stated as a hypothesis for #168, not adjudicated here.

**Consequence for the reframing as a whole, not just the mass test:** the "Euclidean cap" of the framework's own fixed point is not yet a semiclassical object for realistic field content. The reframing's other items — Osterwalder–Schrader reconstruction across the derived junction, WKB time on the branches, the junction-selection question — all presuppose a trustworthy S⁴ cap glued at a K = 0 equator. Either the field content is large (then say so as an input), or the operative junction is the observed-Λ one (then the whole H_J-anchored discussion, including the March synthesis's m_eff ~ √ξ H₀, moves sixty orders and the required exponents become the small rationals 1/6–1/4 of Weinberg–Zel'dovich-type relations), or the cap is a Planckian object and the semiclassical reframing does not apply to it. This should be settled before the junction-selection or OS work is specified.

A third, smaller shared premise: all three attempts treated "mass" as particle rest mass. The co-emergence thesis needs only *a* mass to activate — m_eff ~ √(12(ξ − 1/6)) H_J at p = 1/2 (`conj:mass_generation`) — and the mass *spectrum* was already declared to need additional structure in March. The mass test as posed conflates the two. Attempt B's "two meanings of mass" section is the right disclosure; this synthesis adopts it: the reframing does not threaten the thesis's activation, and it does not touch the spectrum.

## 5. The adaptation: fix a coupling, not a constant

The experimenter's framing was "either the branch structure produces hierarchies, or you need a second scale at the junction, and then you have to say where it comes from." The cycle's answer: the second datum is not a dimensionful constant — adding one (a bare Λ, a membrane charge, a Yukawa) is the input relocated — but a dimensionless coupling, and the mechanism that turns O(1) dimensionless data into a hierarchy without tuning is dimensional transmutation. Candidates, ranked by how much they demand and how much of the record already touches them:

1. **α_{R²}, the R² counterterm coefficient (Sketch for the identification; Rigorous that it is a free modulus in the current record).** The framework's gravitational sector has two dimensionless numbers: a₂ fixes H_J and the cap; α_{R²} fixes the scalaron mass m_s² ∝ M_P²/α_{R²}, the exit from the de Sitter phase (Hawking–Hertog–Reall need d < 0 from an R² counterterm), and therefore the post-instability branch scale — the ~10¹³ GeV Starobinsky scale attempt A filed as "external." It is internal to the framework's own effective action and absent from every enumeration in this cycle until the steelman on A found it. Whether a real, regular K = 0 junction for the anomaly-plus-R² theory constrains α_{R²} is a finite minisuperspace computation nobody has done. **This is the thread-proposal candidate.**
2. **g²(H_J) for an asymptotically free sector (Conjecture that anything at Σ fixes it).** The residue is quantitative: ln(H_J/Λ_QCD) ≈ 40–45. The only rigid Level-0/1 datum found that multiplies 1/g² in the cap's gauge action is the Chern–Simons invariant of a flat connection on the junction 3-manifold (requires π₁(Σ) ≠ 0; the round S³ has none); it weights topological sectors as (Λ_QCD/H_J)^{b₀ cs}, non-negligible for cs ~ 1/60, but does not fix g². Asselmeyer-Maluga–Król (arXiv:1801.10419, full text read by two passes) obtain an electroweak scale exponential in an inverse Chern–Simons invariant of a Brieskorn sphere (exponent 30 vs the needed 40–45); their derivation is unverified and the March synthesis's Verdict 1 on that program stands. A gauge analogue of Di Tucci–Lehners's Robin datum is the input relocated: their Robin term sits on the squared scale factor with one coefficient forced to −6π²i and one new parameter; a boundary term for A_μ is either not gauge-invariant or θ-type, and 1/g² multiplies a bulk term.
3. **A per-species address (the honest residue of C).** If neither (1) nor (2) fixes anything, the ladder's rungs need per-species labels k_s = ½ ln(H_J/m_s), which is the Yukawa sector by another name.
4. **N_inf, the de Sitter depth of the root (cheap, framework-internal, and unexamined).** The number of e-folds the anomaly-driven fixed point lasts before its own instability sets the reach and rung count of any branch-side structure. It is a property of the framework's fixed point, not an import, and no claim node states it.

What is *not* an adaptation: nonzero K_Σ (excluded in every consistent junction on record, §3 item 3); a second dimensionful constant (the input relocated); landscape/anthropic weighting (selection, not derivation — the program should reject it while noting it is the only precedent for no-boundary selection of a modulus, Hartle–Hertog 2013).

## 6. Side findings, independent of the mass test

### 6a. Uniqueness of the toy-model fixed point (Rigorous; closes an open item)

**Claim.** Let G(q) = softmax(c + 2Wq) on the probability simplex Δ, with c = 2 Re(γh) and W = Re(γ(A + βI)), A = Σ_j α_j P_j the marginal-coupling matrix, be the magnitude form of the paper's map F (§Finite toy model; `tests/toy_model.py`). If λ_max(W|_{1⊥}) < 1 — in particular if Re γ < 0 and A + βI ⪰ 0, e.g. α_j ≥ 0, β ≥ 0 (A is PSD for every `dims`, being a sum of block all-ones matrices) — then F has exactly one fixed point in ℂ^N, for any N, any `dims`, any real or complex h, any θ.

**Proof.** E(q) = Φ(q) − cᵀq − qᵀWq with Φ(q) = Σ q_σ ln q_σ. Interior critical points of E on Δ are exactly fixed points of G. On the tangent space Hess Φ = diag(1/q) ⪰ 2I (for Σv = 0: Σv²/q ≥ (Σ|v|)² by Cauchy–Schwarz, and (Σ|v|)² = 4S² ≥ 2‖v‖² with S the sum of positive parts), so Hess E|_T ⪰ 2(I − W|_T) ≻ 0. E is continuous on compact convex Δ and strictly convex, so it has a unique minimizer, interior (∂Φ blows up at the boundary), and no other critical point. Fixed points of F correspond bijectively to fixed points of G via ψ ↦ |ψ|² (every fixed point of F for real γ is a positive real unit vector, Prop. `riem_classical`(a); for complex parameters the phases are functions of q). □

**Prior art (content verified by the steelman on C; the claim node's own recommendation):** Wang, arXiv:2605.15651, Theorem 3, same mechanism ("entropy contributes curvature at least 2 along every tangent direction"). No novelty; the value is that it closes `ce-riem-classical-unique`'s open uniqueness statement under the stated condition. The Lefschetz route in attempt C (all indices +1, index sum 1) is a valid second proof once the attack's three-line tangent-space reduction is written out. Sharpness: the condition is sufficient, not necessary; uniqueness genuinely fails for γ > 0 (up to 15 fixed points found at γ = 0.5, β = 10) and for indefinite A + βI. The lead re-verified the three inequalities numerically on sixty random instances across dimensions.

### 6b. Period cap at 2 for all complex parameters (Rigorous; new to the repo; prior art in the symmetric-network literature)

**Claim C.3.** For arbitrary complex h, α, β, γ and any `dims`, every periodic orbit of F has period 1 or 2, and every ω-limit set consists of fixed points of F∘F. No period-doubling cascade, no period ≥ 3, no invariant circle, at any parameter values.

**Proof sketch (full proof on the scratch branch, steelman-C.md flaw 9).** In magnitudes the map is q_{t+1} = softmax(c + 2Wq_t) with W real symmetric. Since softmax(z) = argmin_p [Φ(p) − pᵀz] (unique, interior), the pair function L(p, q) = Φ(p) + Φ(q) − 2pᵀWq − cᵀ(p + q) satisfies L(G(q), q) ≤ L(p, q) with equality iff p = G(q), and L(p, q) = L(q, p) by symmetry of W. Hence ℓ_t = L(q_{t+1}, q_t) is non-increasing along every orbit, with equality iff q_{t+1} = q_{t−1}; on a periodic orbit it is constant, so the period divides 2. Verified numerically on sixty instances including negative α_j, negative β, γ > 0 and complex parameters (max increase of ℓ_t: 7 × 10⁻¹⁵). □

**Prior art (existence verified; content at abstract level):** Marcus & Westervelt, Phys. Rev. A 40, 501 (1989); Koiran, Neural Computation 6, 459 (1994) — symmetric synchronous networks converge to cycles of length ≤ 2 via a Lyapunov function on consecutive states. C.3 is the softmax/entropy instance. `ce-second-iterate-real-spectrum`'s novelty note ("the nearby literature shows cascades continuing past period 2 in related map classes, so a cap at 2 is not the expected default") has it backwards for *symmetric synchronous* maps: the cap is the expected default and has been a theorem since 1989. The record missed the connection.

**Consequences for the record.** `ce-feigenbaum-cascade` (dead numerically) is dead by theorem. `ce-second-iterate-real-spectrum`'s explanation (real spectrum; non-extension of the congruence to k ≥ 3) is subsumed by a global one that also excludes fold-born period 4 and all odd periods; its positive-semidefiniteness (attempt C's Claim C.1: D(G∘G)|_T is similar to a Gram matrix) is one line from what that node already records, and its bifurcation consequence — a 2-cycle can never flip or Neimark–Sacker — is now a corollary. The corrected bifurcation tree of F for γ < 0, A + βI ⪰ 0: one root (6a); N − 1 flips of the root (0.648020, 1.848777, 2.218503 for the canonical N = 4 instance), each spawning a 2-cycle; fold births of further 2-cycles whose number grows with β (complete census by Lefschetz index sum: 5/13/19/25 at β = 5/10/20/40, of which 1/3/5/7 attracting — the record's "zoo" counts attracting orbits only); every 2-cycle has real nonnegative multipliers; nothing of period ≥ 3. In framework terms: **a reciprocal (symmetric-kernel) self-consistency map cannot branch beyond period 2; branch structure richer than that requires a non-reciprocal kernel, which presupposes an orientation** — attempt C's numerics with non-symmetric kernels (Neimark–Sacker circles, period 3) are exactly what the theorem's negation permits. θ-inertness of the magnitude dynamics holds for the paper's real parameters (the bifurcation structure of F is signature-blind); for complex h the magnitude fixed point moves with θ but the class, hence 6a and 6b, is unchanged.

### 6c. Signature-change boundary: the smooth-class junction condition (Rigorous given the fixed background; informs SCB-6)

For the expanding-region note's profile λ ≃ −c·sgn(x⁰)|x⁰|ⁿ with n odd and a scale factor a(x⁰) smooth in the chart in which the metric is smooth, bounded curvature at Σ (R = 6(ℋ̇ + 2ℋ²) in proper time) requires a − a₀ = O((x⁰)^{n+2}) = O(u²), hence ℋ_Σ = 0, i.e. K_Σ = 0 (sympy-checked on the scratch branch, steelman-A.md Script 2; re-derived by the lead). A bounded nonzero ℋ_Σ would need a′ ~ |x⁰|^{n/2}, a half-integer power, impossible for smooth a. So the note's "bounded proper expansion rate" condition, restricted to smooth metrics, is *equivalent* to Hayward's / Kossowski–Kriele's vanishing-second-fundamental-form condition, and is weaker only for profiles non-smooth in the signature-change chart. The note's remark that de Sitter (ℋ ≡ H₀ ≠ 0) "sits in the regular class" describes such a non-smooth profile: flat-slice de Sitter glued at any surface has K = 3H₀ and lies outside the smooth class; the smooth junction for de Sitter is the closed-slicing waist a = cosh(Hu)/H at u = 0, which is the Hartle–Hawking equator. Proposed disposition: a small clarification to the note's §2 and de Sitter remark via the gated PR path (draft in the cycle's artifacts).
