### Flaw 1 (the hybrid; F > 0 is the HH-sheet condition; VY re-flips by hand; economy fails; J-4 row backwards)
**Verdict: (c) narrower-but-viable, with (d) framing corrections; the attack's structural conclusion — T is a hybrid — is right and is conceded.**

*What the rescue can still say (each point checked):*

2. **In the fourth-order theory both paths fix the Euclidean momentum at the pole for the background.** A fourth-order Lagrangian needs two data at each end. In the FLT gauge q = a², dτ = N_E dt/√q, so pole regularity a′(0) = 1 is q ≈ 2N_E t, i.e. **q̇(0) = 2N_E — exactly DTL's "Euclidean initial momentum"**. T's "sum over regular caps" imposes it sharply; H's Robin term imposes it as a coherent-state smearing of width β. The Dirichlet-versus-Robin contrast of the Einstein–Λ literature therefore does not carry over: the residual background contrast is *sharp regularity versus smeared regularity with a tuned width*. Di Tucci–Lehners 2018, which is precisely a sum over regular metrics ("we will sum over 4-spheres with given boundaries a₀ = 0 and a₁ > 0 and arbitrary radius", verbatim; regularity geometric, no boundary term), obtains the tunneling sign for its preferred contour, keeps "both signs" of a(t) = ±r sin(N_E t/r) "at first", and selects by a stated criterion: "The enhanced saddle point does not obey the correspondence principle: in the limit ℏ → 0, its weighting becomes larger and larger, so that this quantum effect becomes *more* dominant in the classical limit, rather than less" (verbatim, ar5iv this session). The same authors reversed the choice a year later for fluctuation stability. So the sign choice in the background sector is a known trade-off — correspondence principle (T) against fluctuation stability (H) — and not a T-side invention. *(Sketch: the transcription q̇(0) = 2N_E is exact; the DTL-2018 analogy to the fourth-order sum is not a computation.)*

3. **The distinction collapses, and the collapse is the result.** Each path adds exactly one thing: H a background boundary term (Robin; window on β) and gets the field state for free (regular modes on the HH sheet are the Euclidean vacuum — Hartle–Hawking's classic result); T nothing in the background beyond the regularity both share, plus a field boundary term (VY; parameter-free) and gets background boundedness for free (Flaw 2, the FLT bound). "Definitional economy" is therefore not an argument for either path; the honest comparison is *which sector each path pays in*. For this program the field-sector premise (J-4) is the one the reframing already needed, and H delivers it as an output while T delivers it as an input; the background-sector boundedness is the one J-1 exposed, and T delivers it as a theorem while H needs a bounded-below completion of the action. That is a real fork with a real cost on each side, and it should be recorded as such rather than as an economy claim.

4. **The J-4 row is backwards (Flaw 13) — conceded, see there.**

One ℤ₂, as the attack says. What "no arrow is added by the contour" needs is FLT's statement that N ∈ (0, ∞) and (−∞, ∞) give the same relevant saddles with the same sign (position §1.1, verbatim from the PRL; the (−∞, ∞) contour is symmetric under N → −N, so the tunneling sign cannot be a time orientation) — Sketch by citation. ### Flaw 15 ("the fixed-N q-integral does not exist" argued from the Euclidean sign of a log divergence)

## Surviving form of Position T (precise enough to be attacked again)

**T′. The no-boundary state is the real-lapse Lorentzian path integral (FLT), with the fourth-order theory's second pole datum supplied by regularity (the sum over regular caps, DTL 2018's setting) and the field state supplied by the Vilenkin–Yamada boundary condition at a → 0.** Its claims:

1. *(Rigorous, verbatim FLT; conditional)* Under the real-lapse, real-q definition every relevant saddle has weight ≤ 1. Hence if the sphere's history is predicted at all it carries the tunneling weight e^{−|I_half|} = e^{+I_half}; the Hartle–Hawking-signed sphere is not merely disfavored but excluded. The Hartle–Hawking sign requires a boundary term with a nonzero real exponent on real configurations (DTL's Robin term). *The fork is therefore "tunneling sphere or no sphere" versus "Robin term with a tuned β window".* Inheritance of the bound by the regular-cap sum is Sketch (Flaw 15); DTL 2018's correspondence-principle selection gives the same sign in Einstein gravity (verbatim).
2. *(Rigorous (numerical), ε = 1; the sphere part analytic)* At the de Sitter data the sphere is the unique saddle that is exactly classical (Re I constant along the history, R(T) = 12 real) and the unique one with real Ostrogradsky data at Σ; the other 17 found saddles are non-classical except P, which is nearly classical, less suppressed than the sphere in the J-1 scheme at a₁ ≥ 3.25, and unranked in any frame-independent scheme.
3. *(Sketch, conditional on 1 and on the relevance of the sphere)* In the measure on real histories (J-1's object) the sphere's history beats the double bubble's by e^{|ΔI|} at every ε > ε_c (ΔI/a = −0.12, −0.30, −1.31, −4.14, −14.4, −33.1 at ε = 0.36, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, J-1 corrected; e^{−2 Re I_half} normalization), and J-1's bound ε < ε_c is lifted *in that measure*. **"Sphere unique dominant at the de Sitter data" does not survive**; it is replaced by "sphere unique classical, dominance among relevant saddles open".
4. *(Rigorous, given the bound; relevance caveat for H)* The tunneling wave function is bounded on real minisuperspace; the Hartle–Hawking one is unbounded along the real regular caps at a₁ = 3 (K₁ → ∞) and along the +1-sheet complex saddles at the de Sitter data (Re I down to −59.5, no floor found), if those saddles are relevant for the Robin contour. This is the honest form of the position's item 4.
5. *(Rigorous (numerical), symbolic)* With the constraint-fixed constant C = +8 the round sphere solves the fixed-N q-equation and the full lapse constraint pointwise in the FLT gauge; regular-cap action differences are finite in that scheme; the position's numbers (C = −8) differ from it by 2a Re Δη, cap-dependent, O(a).
6. *(Rigorous, identity; Sketch, reading)* I(T) is even in T; the tunneling/HH ℤ₂ is the orientation of the 4-volume form on the cap (N_E → −N_E ≡ a → −a), not a time orientation of the branch, because the symmetric lapse contour gives the same sign (FLT). The causal orientation of #216 §6d is separate and coincides with the pole-to-Σ direction of N > 0.
7. *(Sketch)* The field-state prescription once the hybrid is conceded: VY's boundary condition at a → 0, parameter-free (h_n = −in/a), selecting mode functions singular at the pole of the tunneling saddle; available for the conformal scalar (exact, VY-II), claimed for gravitational waves (VY 2018 App. A), contested by FLT (App. B), unconstructed for the fourth-order tensor modes. Under T′ the cap's Euclidean vacuum is an input to J-4, not an output.
8. *(Sketch)* The scalar negative-mode window 0 < ε < 1/4 is harmless for T′ and problematic for H (the sphere is a multi-negative-mode saddle of the HH state there).

**The honest comparison that replaces "definitional economy":** each path adds one boundary term — H in the background sector (Robin, β ∈ (β_min, β_max)), getting the field state for free; T′ in the field sector (VY, parameter-free), getting background boundedness for free. The program must decide which sector it wants derived.

**The deciding computation:** the two-dimensional Picard–Lefschetz analysis over (lapse, c₃) at the de Sitter data for the real-lapse contour, ε = 1, as a function of a₁ along the history — determining (i) whether the sphere's −1-sheet thimble is relevant and (ii) whether P (and the non-classical competitors E, D, J, B) are relevant — together with the Herzog–Huang–Jensen boundary terms that make cut-cap actions frame-independent so the survivors can be ranked. If (i) fails, T′ dies as "no sphere", not as "HH sphere". If (ii) puts P in the relevant set with |Re I_P| < |I_half|, the tunneling state at these data is dominated by a fuzzy history on de Sitter's stable manifold rather than by de Sitter itself — a different and interesting outcome, not the double bubble's.
