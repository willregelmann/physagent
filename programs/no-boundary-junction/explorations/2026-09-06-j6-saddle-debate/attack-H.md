- **Severity:** structural.

### 7. §1.3, item 1 — "Gaussian-suppressed perturbations" under the HH sign is transplanted from Einstein–Λ; in this theory the sphere has extra scalar negative modes for 0 < ε < 1/4, most of the window the position defends.
