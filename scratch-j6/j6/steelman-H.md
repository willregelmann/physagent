### 2. "No separate lapse integral remains" confuses saddle-point evaluation with the definition of the integral [lead's Flaw 1, second half]
**Verdict: framing, with a solvable gap the position mislabelled as structure.**
The attack is right. Position H's "the lapse integral's job has already been done by the contour" is withdrawn; the honest statement is that the saddle *set* is known at each (q₁, R₁) and its *relevance* is the Conjecture.
